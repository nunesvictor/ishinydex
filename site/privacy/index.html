iShinyDex privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

  • iShinyDex has no server, no accounts and collects no data.
  • Your data stays on your device.
  • Dropbox sync is optional and stores one file in your own Dropbox, in a folder dedicated to the app.

What iShinyDex is

A free, open-source app to organize a personal Pokémon collection in the style of Pokémon HOME boxes. It runs entirely in the browser: the pages are static files, and none of your information is sent to the project's maintainers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (dexes, boxes, specimens, trainers, saves and settings) is kept in the browser storage on this device (on iPhone and iPad, in the storage of the Home Screen app). Clearing the site data in the browser, or removing the app, deletes it.

Under Settings → Your data you can export everything to a .json file and import it back. The file stays wherever you save it.

Dropbox sync (optional)

If you connect Dropbox under Settings → Sync:

  • You sign in on the Dropbox website. The app never sees your password.
  • The app keeps a Dropbox credential (a refresh token) on the device, so it can sync without asking you to sign in again.
  • The app can only access its own folder, inside Apps in your Dropbox. It writes a single file there, ishinydex.json, with the same data as the device. It cannot see or change any of your other files.
  • Permissions requested from Dropbox: read and write files in the app folder (files.content.read, files.content.write) and read the account name (account_info.read), to show which Dropbox you are connected to.
  • Your device talks to Dropbox directly. Nothing goes through iShinyDex servers, because there are none.

Disconnect under Settings → Sync removes the credential from the device. To revoke access for good, use Connected apps in Dropbox. The file in the app folder stays there until you delete it.

Your use of Dropbox is governed by the Dropbox privacy policy.

Other services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app files. Like any website, GitHub may log technical access data (such as IP addresses); see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Pokémon images come from the public PokeAPI/sprites repository, served by raw.githubusercontent.com.

The app uses no tracking cookies, analytics or ads.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the repository history. Questions: project issues on GitHub.
